# SFX Attributions & catalogue

## Synth v2 (in-house DSP)

All `synth` variants below are generated 100% locally by
`npm run sfx` (`scripts/setup-sfx.ts` + `scripts/sfx-synth.ts`) — deterministic
shaped/tonal synthesis in Node, no external samples, no licensing concerns.

## CC0 field recordings (Kenney)

Recorded sound effects from [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl), licensed
CC0 1.0 — free for commercial use, no attribution required. Transcoded
to mono 16-bit WAV 44.1 kHz and peak-normalized by
`scripts/setup-sfx-cc0.ts`; variant folder: `public/sfx/cc0/`.

CC0 packs used: Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Casino Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds, Voiceover Pack.
