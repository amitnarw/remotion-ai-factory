# The Keeper's Last Night

The keeper had kept the light for forty years. Tonight the storm came early, and the lamp was failing.

[A lighthouse on a rocky coast, waves breaking, a stormy night]

He climbed the stairs one last time. His knees ached on every step.

MARA: Dad, come down. The boat can wait.
TOM: No boat waits in a storm like this.

---

(The lamp room, rain running down the glass)

He lit the wick with shaking hands. Far out, a ship turned toward the light.

MARA: You did it.
